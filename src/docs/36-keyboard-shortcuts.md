# Keyboard shortcuts

Settings has a **Keyboard shortcuts** section where you can see every built-in action, rebind it
to a chord of your choosing, and reset it back to the built-in default — all in one searchable
list.

## Opening the dialog

Open **Settings → Keyboard shortcuts → Customize shortcuts…**. A dialog lists every action —
navigation, tabs, selection, file actions, view, and general — grouped the same way as the
quick "?" shortcuts cheat sheet, with each row showing the action and its current chord.

## Rebinding a shortcut

Click a row's chord button — it switches to **"Press a key…"**. Press the new combo you want;
it's captured immediately and the button updates to show it. `Escape` cancels the capture
without changing anything. Rebinding requires a qualifying `Ctrl` or `Alt` modifier (the same
rule macro hotkeys already follow), so a bare letter or `Shift`-only combo won't be accepted — a
short **"Needs Ctrl or Alt…"** hint appears and capture stays open for another try. A row with no
shortcut assigned shows **Click to set…**.

## Conflict warning

If the chord you just captured is already used by another action, a warning appears naming that
other action, with two choices: **Cancel** leaves both shortcuts exactly as they were, or
**Rebind anyway** applies your new chord and clears it from the other action — two actions never
silently end up sharing one chord.

## Resetting

Each row has its own **Reset** button that restores that one action to its built-in default.
**Reset all to defaults** at the top clears every override in one step.

## Live immediately

Every change here is saved and takes effect right away — no restart needed. Rebind an action and
its new chord works on your very next keystroke; clear an action's chord and that shortcut simply
stops firing until you set a new one.

A few keys stay fixed and can't be rebound to effect, because they're context-sensitive rather
than plain shortcuts: **Enter** (open the selected item), **Escape** (clear the selection), and
**?** (open the quick shortcuts cheat sheet). Everything else in the list is fully remappable.
